# Code of conduct

NapPilot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- **Be respectful and kind.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- **Be welcoming.** People of every background and experience level are welcome here, including first-time contributors.
- **Be constructive.** Give feedback that helps, and accept feedback gracefully.
- **Respect privacy.** Never publish other people's private information, such as an address or email, without their
  permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, email **ugurpm@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who don't follow the code of conduct.
